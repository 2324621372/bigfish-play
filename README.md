# BigFish · Pixel Dive

Play: https://2324621372.github.io/bigfish-play/

English by default. Tap 中文 / EN in the top right to switch languages; the choice is remembered.

Drag, WASD or arrow keys to swim; hold Boost / Space to dash. Eat smaller fish, grow and explore five ocean biomes without depth gates. Dodge warning attacks, survive predator chases and battle giant sea bosses. 33 fish species, 12-frame swimming, escalating music and a bilingual collection.

The complete game works offline. Riffle supports native hit feedback on compatible devices. Scores and upgrade coins are local game progress, not a platform leaderboard or wallet.

中文：右上角切换语言；拖动或方向键移动，空格冲刺。自由下潜，无深度上限。

Frozen Doodle Sea v1 remains at /doodle/.

Fonts: Cubic 11, ZCOOL KuaiLe (SIL OFL 1.1). rough.js and Riffle client SDK (MIT).
